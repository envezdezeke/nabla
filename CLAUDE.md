# nabla — RowdyHacks Finance Track

## The challenge
- RowdyHacks Finance Track, run by the Investment Society (UTSA).
- Build an AI that makes portfolio decisions for a $1M book. Judges replay our frozen model through the 30 days after our data ends (~21 trading days). Short window = mostly noise, so favor robust signals over complex models.
- Team: Jesse (finance: factors, risk rules, pitch) and Ezekiel (quant: pipeline, backtester, API, keys).

## Adopted plan: v5 + replay fixes (plan text in `docs/nabla_plan_v5.pdf`)
A regime-aware factor book: five point-in-time factors, rule-based stress and panic flags, a mean-variance optimizer with costs and an equal-weight anchor, entry/exit bands, rebalanced weekly. No options, no RL, no HMM. v5 was written before the replay format; the fixes below override it. Nothing has been run on real data yet.

### Organizer answers
- Output is a **chronological series of target portfolios**, one complete record per decision, generated by the judges **replaying our frozen model through the holdout one step at a time** (proposed format, may still change). Example record fields: `schema_version`, `team_id`, `model_id`, `decision_id`, `information_cutoff` (e.g. 16:00 ET), `decision_time` (16:15), `execution_time` (next open 09:30), `action` ("rebalance"), `target_holdings` [{ticker, weight}]. The example satisfies a 20% per-name cap.
- Cash can be held; scoring will be changed so cash is not punished (current `check.py` still checks sum 1.0 +/- 0.01).
- Scored mostly on total return; Sharpe and drawdown still matter. Transaction costs must be in our model. We set our own limits. Weekly rebalancing allowed.

## Core interface (replay fix)
- One deterministic function `decide(information_cutoff) -> decision record`. It reads only data available at the cutoff **timestamp** (filings, guidance updates and minute bars compared to the time, not just the date). Never `date.today()`, never `max(ds.trading_days())` in the decision path.
- Frozen: everything fitted lives in `config/model.json` (with git commit + data hash); fixed seeds; no refitting or learning during the replay.
- **State rebuild:** the record does not carry previous holdings, but bands, `w_prev` in the cost term, held-names pool, trailing stop and flag hysteresis need it. `decide()` rebuilds it by replaying its own decisions from a fixed start date, unless organizers confirm in-order calls with state allowed.
- The research backtest loop calls the same `decide()` at each historical step, so backtest = replay.
- **Execution at next open:** research returns use fills at the next trading day's open (`open` column), costs charged there. Keep a separate close-to-close function that matches `check.py` for `/backtest`.
- Use `ds.trading_days()` and the holiday calendar for execution dates; timestamps in ET with offset.

## Rules
- Long-only stocks. Stay inside `ds.universe()`. Do not delete sentinel `ORKD`.
- Fundamentals join by filing date/time (`ds.asof()` / `ds.fundamentals(asof=...)`).

## Step 1: regime read (rules, data up to the cutoff)
- **Stress:** SPX below its 200-day average AND (21-day SPX realized vol OR funding stress above its 80th percentile, expanding history).
- **Panic:** SPX 12-month return negative AND 21-day realized vol above its 80th percentile. Only changes the momentum weight.
- Hysteresis: a flag turns off only after two straight weeks of false conditions (rebuilt from data, see state rebuild).

## Step 2: equity scores
- Liquidity filter: 20-day ADV > $50M, price > $5, drop the most illiquid Amihud decile.
- Factors: momentum 12-1 (split/dividend-adjusted), guidance velocity (`guidance_range_velocity`), quality (ROE or gross profit/assets, filed), value (filed TTM EPS/price), volatility premium (-log(IV30/RV21); verify the Bali and Hovakimyan citation).
- Winsorize 1/99, z-score within ~10 SIC-division sector groups, clip +/-3.
- **Composite (fix):** weighted SUM over the fixed five factors with missing = 0, divided by the fixed total weight (not by each stock's available weights, which biases the top toward names with few factors). Momentum weight 0.5 in panic, 1 otherwise.
- Candidate pool: top 25 plus held names still ranked <= 30; max 3 per correlation cluster; crowding check vs a naive 12-month momentum top 20.

## Step 3: risk preset
| Preset | When | Cash floor | Beta band |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | Stress off | 0-5% | 1.10-1.20 |
| Stress | Stress on | 25% | 0.90-1.00, highest-beta names swapped for lower-beta |
If the sum-to-1 check stays, Stress holds low-beta names instead of cash.

## Step 4: optimizer (cvxpy)
- mu_i = beta_shrunk_i * m + lambda_alpha * IC * sigma_cs * score_i; beta_shrunk = 0.67*beta + 0.33; m ~ 0.8% per 21 days. Ledoit-Wolf covariance on 252 daily returns.
- Maximize mu'w - (gamma/2) w'Sw - sum c_i |w_i - w_prev_i| - kappa ||w - w_EW||^2, with mu and costs in the same units (fraction of book).
- Constraints: sum w <= 1 - cash floor; 0 <= w_i <= 10%; sector group <= 30%; beta band in linear form; w_i V <= 1% ADV; earnings-in-window names <= 6%.
- **Anchor (fix):** w_EW = equal weights over the anchor names (top 15 survivors after the 2% drop), written as a linear expression of the invested total sum(w) so the problem stays convex.
- Solve, drop weights < 2%, re-solve on survivors; keep 12-18 names. kappa puts the solution about halfway between the raw optimum and equal weight; gamma targets 18-22% predicted vol.
- Fallback: on solver failure/infeasibility/timeout, equal-weight top 15 with caps.

## Step 5: weekly reassessment
- Entry: a new name enters only if ranked top 15. Exit: a held name leaves only if ranked below 30. The bands define the pool; the optimizer can still give a held name zero weight, so churn control rests on the cost term (state this in AUDIT.md).
- Go directly to the optimizer target; skip changes under 2 points. Trim only above 12%, back to 10%. Earnings: keep the 6% cap through a report, do not add the week before (note: `days_to_next_report` is a projection).
- Optional trailing stop: exit if 15% below high since entry and ranked worse than 20.

## Costs
Half-spread 5 / 10 / 20 bps by liquidity tercile plus impact k * Amihud * dollars traded (k = 1; Amihud is x10^6 scaled). Rerun at 2x costs.

## Validation
- Backtest = weekly replay of `decide()` from 2018 to the cutoff, next-open fills, costs charged. Walk-forward only; labels lag 21 trading days; last six months untouched until the go/no-go.
- Metrics vs SPX and an equal-weight liquid universe: total/annualized return, Sharpe, max drawdown, turnover, cost paid; rolling 21-day returns (median, 5th, 95th, worst); 2018 Q4, 2020, 2022.
- Ablation ladder (each rung must beat the previous after costs): 1 v3 equal-weight top 15 with limits and bands; 2 panic momentum weight; 3 rule-based cash dial; 4 optimizer with anchor.
- Leak tests: shuffled labels, one-day delay, drop-one-factor. Survivorship stated in AUDIT.md.
