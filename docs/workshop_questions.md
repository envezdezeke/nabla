# Organizer questions

Already answered, so do not re-ask:
- Output is a chronological series of target portfolios, generated by replaying our frozen model through the holdout one step at a time (proposed format).
- Cash can be held, and weights sum to 1 including cash.
- Scored mostly on total return; Sharpe and drawdown still matter.
- Transaction costs must be in our model. We set our own limits. Weekly rebalancing allowed.
- Data stops at the cutoff; the test is the 30 days after it.
