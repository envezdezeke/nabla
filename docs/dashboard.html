How a decision is made

The same frozen function runs every week, in our backtest and in the judges' replay.

Read

Only data known by 16:00 ET on the decision day. Filings count from the day after they are filed.

Filter

Liquid names only: over $50M traded a day, price above $5, least liquid tenth dropped.

Score

Momentum, guidance, quality and value, each ranked against similar stocks, averaged with fixed weights.

Pick

Top 15 at equal weight. Caps: 10% a stock, 30% a group, 6% near earnings, book beta at most 1.2.

Protect

If the S&P is below its 200-day average and volatility is high, 25% moves to cash.

The record the judges receive

One complete target portfolio per decision. Weights sum to 1; cash, when held, is the CASHHOLDING line.
